# TechOps Hero · Story bible addendum

## The ascent and K’s face

Red has his established face throughout his life in the Low Quarter, the crossing, and the first part of his captivity. The workers he freed at the relay vault gave him the name El Mago Rojo. During the ascent, the robe and ivory mask give him a chance to try that name on his own terms. He lifts the mask away. The face beneath it follows the creator’s portrait, with dark eyes visible through square black frames, close curls and a connected beard and moustache.

Volume II, collected page 80, shows the change in flashback. Page 81 returns K to Cell 118. In story time the encounter precedes his meeting with Waldo in the yard, so K already has this face in Volume I. His name, memories and choices belong to him. Mike’s voice is familiar to him, and Mike keeps his own likeness.

## Waldo’s home on Earth

Waldo and K do their independent IT and radio work at Waldo’s house. The workshop opens onto the yard, within sight of the porch and the van. It holds their tools, cassette deck, bench radio and local relay. The antenna is mounted on the property. Mike and Felicia can visit, and AeroTech staff can call from their shift.

Waldo texts Mike after landing and invites him over. He takes off his wrestling mask at home. Mike knew Waldo as a child and meets K for the first time in the garage.

The prison relay sent copies of Mike’s memories to K. Mike can pause the incoming transfers. Felicia’s recording identifies the nearby transmitter, which they isolate. K can then disconnect his own data lead and keep the cassettes. Dee and Priya have a separate decision at AeroTech. Orpheus can flag a fault on the test rig. Checking the equipment and clearing a restart take two people.

The salvaged relay has separate data and audio connections. K and Mike remove the control path when the prison box requests a response. The surviving audio connection gives K and Iona a chance to speak across dimensions.

## People and props

- Felicia works in security research and brings her own signal recording. A separate approach controller clears the shuttle for landing.
- Iona lives in the Low Quarter. On Earth, K can remember her or hear her through the link. Her recollections, performances and end of the radio conversation are shown in their own panels.
- In the escape, Waldo keeps his red-and-black mask and leather jacket. K wears his dark hooded layers, black knit cap and headphones. His embroidered K belongs on his outer sleeve. Katrin and Manchez keep their blue and amber gear.
- At the home workbench, the capsule beacon places K on Earth before Day One. The later station intake is a separate record. The hiss belongs to the tape. K plays the last beat live.
