K Terminal — Music, Comics & Worlds

K Terminal brings together original music, comics, artwork and playable worlds by Mike Olivares, creating as Defied Stars. Start with a song, open the comic, or walk through the gallery.

Music that travels with you

The terminal plays the @raikouno SoundCloud catalogue. Choose a track and turn background listening on to keep the same music playing as you read. Page-guided listening becomes available when song cues are assigned; you can define a local preview.

TechOps Hero

Read the illustrated comic in its dedicated reader, with edition selection, page navigation and bookmarks saved in your browser. K, Mike, Iona, Waldo and their connected stories also inspire the game and music.

See World and the game

See World explores character perspectives linked to TechOps Hero playthroughs. Story availability depends on progress in the matching game save. A new device or a different website address may have different saved data. Read about save recovery before starting over.
