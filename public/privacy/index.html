K TERMINAL

Privacy

Updated November 9, 2026

K Terminal is operated by Mike Olivares / Defied Stars. This page explains the data used by the site’s current features.

Data saved in your browser

Comic bookmarks, music libraries, audio preferences, soundtrack drafts and feedback drafts can be stored on your device using local storage. TechOps Hero and See World also store game data in your browser. Storage is specific to the browser, device and website origin; changing domains does not move it automatically.

These features do not provide a cloud backup. Clearing site data or using private browsing can remove it. Export a backup before clearing data. The recovery tool lets you explicitly export and import selected reading and music settings. It does not migrate game identities or story awards.

Advertising and embedded services

AdSense is disabled across this site, including the portfolio and case studies. No advertising consent is requested while advertising is disabled.

The interactive app requests fonts from Google Fonts. SoundCloud’s embedded player and catalogue requests contact SoundCloud and its delivery providers; SoundCloud may use cookies or other identifiers under its own policy. GitHub Pages serves the site and may process connection information such as your IP address. The recovery pages load no music, fonts or advertising from third parties.

See SoundCloud’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement for their practices.

Feedback

Draft text stays in this browser until you choose to share it. Copying a message does not send it. Instagram messages use your Instagram account; GitHub feedback is public and associated with your GitHub account after you submit it there. Do not include private information in public comments.

Questions and choices

You can remove local data through your browser’s site settings, after making any backups you need. For questions, contact @defied_stars on Instagram or use the project feedback links. Only share information needed for your question.
