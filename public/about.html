About K Terminal

K Terminal is Mike Olivares’s personal creative space, bringing together original comics, music, artwork and interactive experiences. TechOps Hero is its connected story universe, with a comic reader, music by @raikouno and places to explore.

Defied Stars is the longer-term creative vision behind this work: original projects and fairly supported collaborations with artists, animators, musicians and developers. The aim is to help sustain that work through advertising while keeping the creative experience accessible.

Start with the TechOps Hero comic, browse the music in K Terminal, or visit the media hub. For collaboration enquiries or questions about the project, use the Contact page.
