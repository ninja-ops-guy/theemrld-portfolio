Privacy at K Terminal

Updated November 11, 2026

K Terminal is operated by Mike Olivares. This page explains how information is handled when you use the comic reader, music player and interactive experiences.

Information stored in your browser

The site uses browser storage to remember features such as your comic bookmark, playlists, playback preferences, guided soundtrack drafts, feedback drafts and available story progress. These records are associated with the browser and website origin where they were created. Clearing site data may remove them. Use an available export feature before clearing progress you want to keep.

Music and other external services

The music player uses SoundCloud. Loading its player connects your browser to SoundCloud, which handles information under its own privacy policy. Some pages load fonts from Google. The site and some downloadable files are delivered through GitHub services. Those providers receive the information needed for your browser to request their resources, including your IP address and request details. See Google’s privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Feedback

A feedback draft is saved in your browser when storage is available. Choosing to review it on GitHub sends the prepared text to GitHub’s issue form. It becomes a public issue only when you submit it there. Do not put sensitive information in a public issue.

Google advertising

K Terminal has integrated Google AdSense. When its advertising code runs, Google and participating advertising providers may use cookies, similar technologies, IP addresses and device identifiers to deliver and measure advertising and detect fraud. When personalized advertising is enabled with the required choices, advertisements may reflect visits to this and other websites. Learn more in Google’s explanation of data use on partner sites and in its privacy policy.

You can manage Google’s advertising personalization through Google Ads Settings. Turning off personalization does not necessarily stop every advertising-related use of cookies or other information. Browser settings also provide cookie and site-data controls.

Contact

For questions about this site’s handling of information, contact Mike Olivares through the Contact page. Each external service provides its own privacy information and controls.
