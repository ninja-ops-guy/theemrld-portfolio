Privacy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

K Terminal and the THEEMRLD portfolio at kterminal.site are operated by Mike Olivares. This page describes browser storage and third-party services used by the site, including its music and comic experiences.

Browser storage and saved settings

The site uses browser storage to remember preferences, music selections and playlists, comic reading positions, and project-comment drafts. These settings belong to the browser and site address where you save them. Clearing this site's data in your browser can remove them.

Connected experiences, including the separately hosted TechOps Hero game, can use their own storage. Clearing data for kterminal.site does not necessarily clear data saved at another site address. Settings recovery is initiated by you; it does not transfer game saves or identities.

Advertising

This site includes Google AdSense. Google and other third-party advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses and device or browser identifiers to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and, where enabled and permitted, personalize advertising based on visits to this site and other websites.

Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this and other sites. You can manage personalized advertising through Google My Ad Center and learn about participating vendors' opt-out choices at YourAdChoices. These choices do not necessarily stop all advertising or apply across every browser and device.

See how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy for details.

Hosting, fonts, music and linked services

When your browser requests a page, font, image, download, embedded player or advertisement, the service receiving that request can receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details and the requested resource. Services used by this site include:

  • GitHub: website hosting, source code, media releases, downloads and project comments. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts: font delivery for the interactive site. See Google Fonts privacy information.
  • SoundCloud: embedded music playback and related track information. SoundCloud's player may use its own cookies and storage. See the SoundCloud Privacy Policy.

External profiles and other destinations have their own privacy practices. Review their policies when you use those services.

Project comments

The project-comment form saves your draft in this browser. Choosing to continue on GitHub transfers the draft to GitHub in a prefilled issue URL. Publishing the issue is a separate action on GitHub; published comments can be public and associated with your GitHub account. Avoid including private information in a draft you choose to transfer or publish.

Your choices and contact

You can clear or block browser storage and cookies using your browser settings. This can reset saved preferences or affect playback and other features. Advertising choices provided by Google and other vendors are linked above.

For questions about this site or this privacy notice, contact Mike Olivares on LinkedIn. LinkedIn's own privacy practices apply when you use that service. Do not post private information in public project issues.
