A world you can walk through.

WASD walks; drag the view to turn. Arrow keys turn or walk. E interacts with an artwork, terminal, vehicle or gate. T opens K Terminal without moving your camera. Escape closes overlays.

On touch screens, hold the direction buttons and drag the scene with your other hand. The action button appears when you approach a work or doorway.

Every realm has its own geometry and a marked return gate. Use the Atlas to travel directly. Inside K-01 Orbital, F starts EVA; Space climbs, C descends, and F lands safely. On the Moon, Space jumps with low gravity.

Scene changes queue the next soundtrack; the current song plays to its actual end. Pause and manual song selection always win. SoundCloud may require one explicit Play gesture. The public widget does not expose audio samples or playback speed. Local audio enables genuine FFT response and speed adjustment.

The gallery’s cedar, bronze, twin pillars, palms and pomegranates are a Solomon-inspired artistic interpretation—not a reconstruction of an ancient building.
