## Volume II / celestial ascent and face

- K's face beneath the ivory mask follows the creator portrait: dark eyes behind square black frames, close curls, connected beard and moustache. The green-and-gold hood/robe and mask belong to the ascent reveal. This is the same person as Red/K/El; no clone or new identity.
- Story chronology: the ascent face reveal is shown in Volume II collected page 80; page 81 returns to Cell 118. In story time the encounter precedes K's meeting with Waldo in the yard, so K already has this face in Volume I. Mike keeps his own likeness. The event does not alter K's name, memories, choices, or Mike's separate identity.
- Older panels that conflict are review evidence, not authority to silently revert the face. Mark unresolved image-level appearance questions as uncertain if exact approved creator portrait is not present.

## Waldo's Earth home and workshop

- Waldo/Oz and K conduct independent IT and radio work at Waldo's house. The workshop opens onto the yard within sight of porch and van; tools, cassette deck, bench radio and local relay are present; antenna is on the property.
- After landing, Waldo texts Mike and invites him over. Waldo removes his wrestling mask at home. Mike, who knew Waldo as a child, first meets K in the garage. Don't move their first meeting earlier.
- Keep Mike→K memory feed distinct from K's own memories. Mike may pause incoming transfers; Felicia's recording identifies a nearby transmitter; they isolate it; K may unplug his own data lead and retain the cassettes. Dee and Priya's AeroTech decision is separate. A test-rig restart requires two-person verification. Salvaged relay data/control and audio paths are distinct; removing control path can leave a speaker connection for K/Iona.

## Iona, places, props, wardrobe

- Iona lives in the Low Quarter. Earth-side panels may show K remembering her or hearing her through the link. Her Low Quarter recollections, performances and the end of the radio conversation must have their own panels and must not imply she is physically at Waldo's Earth workshop.
- In the prison escape, Waldo wears his red-and-black mask and leather jacket; K wears dark hooded layers, black knit cap and headphones. His embroidered K belongs on his outer sleeve. Katrin and Manchez retain their distinct blue and amber gear from established canon.
- At Waldo's home workbench, the capsule beacon places K on Earth before Day One; the later station intake is a separate record. The hiss belongs to the tape; K plays the last beat live.
