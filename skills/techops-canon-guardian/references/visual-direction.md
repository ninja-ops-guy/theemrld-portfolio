# Visual direction and uniformity system

## Medium-specific hard style lock
- **Reader comic:** cinematic inked/industrial present, legible facial acting and compositions, grounded interfaces, material textures, consistent line weights/palette across adjacent objective pages. **2:3 portrait page** (`width:height=2:3`), stable gutters, no cut-off captions, bubbles, faces or hands. No stretched figure crops. Proof on a phone.
- **Game:** established **pixel-art** visual language; re-compose comic images for pixel art rather than pasting painterly frames or changing character models. Interactive computer visuals remain readable and diegetic.
- **Subjective Red/Iona memory:** painterly textures, warmer reds and softened field edges. Memories are emotionally true but not automatically historically complete; no documentary timestamp without source.
- **ORPHEUS/Warden claims or counterfactual:** clinical black/white symmetric frames; visually mark claims provisional rather than presenting them as verified reality.
- **Corroboration:** when an external tape, physical relay record, log, or testimony verifies one detail, cut to sharp evidence inset that limits the claim to the actual record.
- **Celestial event:** distinct, stable realm register, not the memory blur. Never imply heaven is imaginary simply because it shares a motif with a flashback.

## Color / shape identity
- **Cyan:** Mike's verified ops / trustworthy tools.
- **Purple:** Felicia's signal, music, ambiguity and partnership.
- **Red:** Red/K's past, reflection, authorship and Cell 118, **not evil**.
- **Amber:** aerospace hardware, machinery, maintenance and physical work.
- **Emerald:** angular geometric field phenomena of K; keep shapes distinct from small rounded dog trust cues and controlled MORNINGSTAR swarm signals.
- **Black/white:** bounded ORPHEUS/Warden administrative abstractions, not proof.

## Persistent style header to prepend to artwork prompts
> TECHOPS HERO visual canon. Medium: [comic/game]. Art bible: [version]. Same named actors and approved model-sheet references as prior approved pages. Preserve face likeness, hair silhouette, costume and accessories; no substitutions. Industrial cinematic ink / documentary machines in objective present; switch to painterly warm memory or clinical provisional claims **only as scene_channel requires**. Maintain 2:3 portrait page, gutters, text-safe zones, legible on mobile. Stable camera geography, character handedness, eye line and prop positions across panel sequence. Do not invent logos, song credits or new lore. Scene register: [channel]. Color accents: [character-specific].

## Per-panel continuity evidence
Record reference filename + hash, output filename + hash, actor/costume state, environment orientation, relative date, camera axis and scene channel. Keep an approved per-character **face crop** or model sheet and compare every close-up side-by-side. Character sheet must take precedence over fuzzy remembered renders. When a correction replaces one panel, compare before/after and ensure it does not silently alter other panels.

## Composition and craft checks
Facial resemblance; distinctive hair; costume and accessory consistency; physical pose; seated driver side and steering-wheel direction; hands and number of fingers; near/far shoulder; car travel / jump trajectory; 180° axis; monitor UI legibility; page/caption safe areas; consistent phonetic names; speaker tails; type hierarchy; daylight/night lighting; no background/extra-character duplication; no panel or page repeats. If reference art or the actual page is missing, verdict is NOT VERIFIED.
