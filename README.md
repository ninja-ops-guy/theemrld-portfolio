# THEEMRLD — Systems & Security Engineering Portfolio

A portfolio focused on **inspectable engineering evidence** across infrastructure, security, automation, applied AI, research software, and product engineering.

The projects here are not presented as equally mature products. Each case study is labeled by its actual state — live, active development, experimental, or research — and emphasizes the problem, implementation, verification, and remaining work.

## Portfolio Thesis

I build systems that operate, test, or improve other systems.

That includes production-minded software, automated QA, security experimentation, AI orchestration, research tooling, and infrastructure-focused automation. The goal of this portfolio is to make that work easy to evaluate without relying on résumé claims alone.

## Flagship Research & Engineering

### RESIDUAL — Trustworthy Agent Systems / Verification

The center of the current portfolio. RESIDUAL treats autonomous workers as potentially stochastic or wrong and moves trust into the surrounding system: contracts, bounded execution, evidence receipts, independent verification, deterministic integration, and explicit failure states.

- Source: https://github.com/ninja-ops-guy/residual-agent-harness
- Status: **Public R&D / active evaluation**

### RAC / Adversarial Clothing — Adversarial ML / Physical Robustness

An evidence-gated research platform for physically realizable adversarial fashion. Digital capability and physical efficacy are deliberately separated; frozen protocols, provenance, negative-result retention, and held-out evaluation protect that boundary.

- Source: https://github.com/ninja-ops-guy/adversarial-clothing-pipeline
- Status: **Public R&D; physical P1 pending**

### Production Systems Engineering — Operational Foundation

The research portfolio is grounded in production operations: incident ownership, infrastructure recovery, networking, automation, observability, and reducing recurring operational work. Sanitized case studies connect that experience to the verification and reliability concerns that appear throughout the research projects.

### Vector / WirePod — Embodied AI / Verified Robot Control

A private embodied-agent research platform built around typed LLM control, reusable robot skills, event-driven coordination, and a developing RESIDUAL qualification layer for generated behavior.

- Status: **Active R&D / private; public release planned**

Private and commercially oriented work is intentionally not used as the primary evidence layer when the implementation cannot be inspected publicly.

## What to Evaluate

When reviewing the portfolio, the strongest signals are:

1. **Architecture** — how problems are decomposed into systems and interfaces.
2. **Verification** — tests, CI, benchmarks, runtime checks, and failure handling.
3. **Iteration** — evidence that failures lead to concrete engineering changes.
4. **Operational thinking** — deployment, reliability, safety boundaries, observability, and maintainability.
5. **Technical range** — infrastructure, security, automation, AI systems, research software, and interactive products.
